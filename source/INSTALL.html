KUBLA KHAN 2.1.8

Real-browser tested with isolated classroom fixtures. Your signed-in Khan classroom remains unverified; no course-credit claim is made.
  1. Stop and remove the old KUBLA KHAN in chrome://extensions.
  2. Extract the ZIP into a permanent folder.
  3. Enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select extension.
  4. Confirm version 2.1.8, pin the icon, and refresh the classroom tab.
  5. Open All assignments, choose To-do, then click Start both tabs.
  6. Use Stop all to cancel future actions. For a pause/failure, use Copy diagnostics.

Supported problems are automatically entered and checked. Unsupported or uncertain items pause. Keep the extracted folder; Chrome loads it directly.
